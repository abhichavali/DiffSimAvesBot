* The free airframe (~src/flying.py~)

The compiled scene is a bench rig: fixed struts and motor pillars are welded to the
world and only the mobile struts (wings) float, held by the tendon net.
~flying.to_airframe~ reparents every world-welded body under one ~airframe~ body on
a ~<freejoint>~; the mobile struts keep their own freejoints and stay coupled only
through the tendons. The pillars fly massless and each crank disk carries 2.5 g for
its motor, so the whole bird weighs 226 mN (~FLIGHT_WEIGHT_RANGE_N~ = 200-250 mN; the
~__main__~ self-check asserts it). The seed's bench lift at its own drive is 187-210 mN
(~219 mN with the stroke plane leveled), below that weight.

Scene helpers (pure XML surgery): ~shrink_pillar_visual~ (cosmetic),
~enable_wing_collision~, ~add_poles~, ~add_ceiling~, ~add_mocap_obstacle~,
~add_visual_bird~. Lessons that each cost a broken demo:
- Collision and skin geoms must be massless. A default-density wing collider weighed
  ~26 g per wing and the mesh skin ~2.3 kg; both sank the bird.
- Collision masks are asymmetric so a wing hits only the obstacle (wing
  ~contype 2 / conaffinity 4~, obstacle the reverse): a shared bit made the two wings
  hit each other and the struts at the top of the stroke.
- Obstacles are mocap bodies: kinematic, placed at runtime, weightless to the bird.

* What was learned about free hover (September 2026, current)

- The free bird needs body inertia (~flying.BODY_INERTIA~, same mass): without it the
  airframe recoils against every wingbeat and the lift is gone (-15 mN free vs 239 mN
  held). It hovers pitched ~21 deg.
- The cranks must be phase-locked: plain velocity servos let the t1/t4 lag drift
  ~0.05-0.07 deg per wingbeat, which slides the bird out of trim.
- Launch through a cradle that holds only the wingbeat-averaged pose; releasing from
  rest tumbles it.
- The trimmed open loop is unstable (per-wingbeat |eig| ~1.08; lost after ~40-50
  wingbeats); an LQR on the one-wingbeat linear model, distilled to an MLP, hovers.
- BPTT through MJX still diverges past one wingbeat; one-wingbeat ~jacfwd~ is accurate.
- Sensors (~hover.sensors~, ~hover.reduced~; findings ~2026-09-26-hover-minimal-inputs~ and
  ~2026-09-26-hover-imu-noise~): with mocap position plus the IMU, an LQG on ~pos,att,w~
  designed with the datasheet noise needs no velocity and hovers level (drift ~15 mm, all
  pushes, also with 5 ms mocap latency). Without position the bird holds attitude but walks
  0.3-0.75 m in 14 s, sinks, and hovers tilted by the accelerometer bias (~1.4 deg at 20 mg).

* What was learned about free hover (July 2026, pre-2026-09 aero)

- Open loop the free body tumbles 90-180 deg within ~15 wingbeats at every timestep
  tried (0.13-0.40 ms). A cycle-averaged linear policy over crank speed and
  stroke-plane authority could not catch it.
- Pitching the stroke plane (disk pitch 20 deg) pumps a constant forward thrust;
  leveling it makes the stroke vertical.
- With the wrench, attitude authority was the lever: a weakly held body leaned ~56 deg,
  vectored its lift sideways and drifted a metre; holding it within ~4 deg held
  position within centimetres.
- Reverse-mode BPTT through the stiff flapping rollout exploded (gradients ~1e11) at
  every horizon and starting point tried.

* The renders (~src/demos/~, headless MP4)

| script                                  | what it shows                                                    |
|-----------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ~render_configs.py --config flying~     | the free airframe flapping, skinned                              |
| ~render_configs.py --config multimodal~ | a precessing stroke plane, per-blade aero arrows                 |
| ~render_configs.py --config collision~  | poles on the flight path: each wing strike knocks the drive down, sweeps the wings aft and spools back up |
| ~render_configs.py --config ceiling~    | the same beat against a slab 65 mm above the stroke              |
| ~render_flight_phases.py~               | hover -> cruise -> glide: stroke-plane pitch 0 -> 30 -> 90 deg, motor fade |
| ~render_stroke_plane.py~                | stroke-plane tilt/pitch and drive-speed sweep, body-frame wingtip trail |

Each has ~--self-check~ (asserts its schedule, no sim); ~render_configs --dry-run~
runs the physics without rendering. The collision and ceiling reports assert real
wing contacts, so they fail while the bird cannot fly to the obstacles.
